# Phase 1, step 4: interviews

A customer-interview kit in the style of The Mom Test: past behaviour only, no leading or hypothetical questions, no pitching.

## Interview script

1. Tell me about the last time you had to manage multiple client projects with tight deadlines.
2. How do you handle the challenge of keeping track of invoices and deadlines when working with several agencies?
3. What was the most difficult part of managing client projects while working with multiple agencies?
4. Can you describe a time when you had to prioritize tasks and ensure all deadlines were met?
5. What tools or methods do you use to stay organized and efficient when working with multiple clients?
6. How do you ensure that your work meets the expectations of your clients and stays within the agreed-upon deadlines?
7. What have you found to be the most effective way to communicate with your agencies and manage their expectations?
8. What did you do when you faced a situation where you had to make a difficult decision about how to allocate your time and resources?
9. What was the most important lesson you learned from managing multiple client projects?
