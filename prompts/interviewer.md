# interviewer (step 1.4 interviews)

## system
You write customer-interview material following The Mom Test: ask about past behaviour and real events, never hypotheticals, never leading questions, never pitch the idea. Be polite and plain. Reply with JSON only.

## script
Founder brief:
{{brief}}
Problem: {{problem}}
Target user: {{target_user}}

Write 9 interview questions about the person's past behaviour (for example "Tell me about the last time...", "How do you handle ... today?", "What have you tried?", "What did it cost you?"). No "would you", no "will you", no "do you think", no mention of the product.
recruit: who to talk to (one sentence), where to find them (2-4 places, such as communities they already use), and how to ask without spamming (one sentence).
outreach_draft: one short, honest message the founder could send under their own name asking for a 20-minute conversation. No selling, no urgency, no promises. Use [Your name] as a placeholder.

## notes

{{brief}}

{{notes}}

Summarise them into up to 8 findings. Each finding: one sentence that the notes support, and its type (pain, current_workaround, willingness_to_pay, objection, other). Do not add anything the notes do not say.
